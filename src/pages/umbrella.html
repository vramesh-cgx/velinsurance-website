Umbrella insurance

An extra million dollars of protection

Umbrella coverage pays when a lawsuit is bigger than your auto or home liability limit.

Ask about umbrella
How it works

It sits on top of your other policies

If a serious car accident leads to a $700,000 judgment and your auto policy pays $250,000, a $1 million umbrella covers the other $450,000, plus legal defense.

Who should consider it

  • Homeowners with savings or retirement accounts to protect
  • Families with teenage drivers
  • Owners of rental property, pools or trampolines
  • Anyone whose net worth is above their liability limits

Umbrella policies require minimum limits on your auto and home. We set those up together, so there are no gaps.

Questions

Before you send your policy

What should I send?

Your current declarations page for each policy: the page that lists your coverages, drivers, vehicles and premium. Your insurance company's app or website usually has it under Documents. For auto, a photo of each driver's license helps.

Will getting a quote hurt my credit score?

No. Insurance companies use a soft inquiry for quotes, which does not affect your credit score.

Do I have to switch?

No. If your current policy is already the best value, we tell you, and you keep it.

When should I switch?

Usually on your renewal date, so you don't pay a cancellation fee or lose a paid-in-full discount. We line the new policy up so there is no gap in coverage.

Is my information safe?

Uploads go straight to our agency's private Google Drive and are only used to quote your insurance. Never send credit card or bank details with your documents.

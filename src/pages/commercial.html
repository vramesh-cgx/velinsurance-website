Common coverage for small businesses

CoverageWhat it pays for
General liabilityA customer or visitor is hurt, or you damage someone else's property, because of your work
Business owner's policy (BOP)General liability and your business property together in one policy, usually for less than buying them separately
Commercial autoVehicles owned by the business or used for work, which a personal auto policy may not cover
Workers' compensationMedical bills and lost wages when an employee is hurt on the job. Georgia requires it once you have three or more employees, including part-time.
Professional liabilityClaims that your advice or service caused a client a financial loss (also called errors and omissions)
Commercial umbrellaExtra liability above your general liability, auto and workers' comp limits

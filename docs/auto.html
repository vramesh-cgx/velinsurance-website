The coverages that matter after an accident

Georgia's legal minimum is 25/50/25: $25,000 per person and $50,000 per accident for injuries, and $25,000 for property damage. One serious accident can cost far more, so most of our clients carry 100/300/100 or higher.

CoverageWhat it pays for
Bodily injuryInjuries you cause to other people
Property damageOther people's cars and property
Uninsured motoristYour injuries and car when the other driver has too little insurance. In Georgia, choose added on rather than reduced by, so it stacks on top of the other driver's limits.

Ways to pay less

  • Bundling auto with home or renters
  • Multiple cars on one policy
  • Good student (B average or better)
  • Georgia defensive driving course
  • Paying in full and going paperless
  • Several years with no claims or tickets

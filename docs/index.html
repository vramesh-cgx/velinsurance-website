Common questions

Will a quote affect my credit?

No. Insurance quotes use a soft check that does not affect your credit score.

What do I need to get started?

A few details about your home or cars, or a photo of your current policy's declarations page.

Is switching a hassle?

We set up the new policy and cancel the old one for you, timed so there is no gap in coverage.

Do I have to switch?

No. If your current policy is already the best value, we tell you, and you keep it.

Is my information safe?

Uploads go straight to our agency's private Google Drive and are only used to quote your insurance. Never send credit card or bank details with your documents.
